A Practical Paper-Reading Workflow

Paper Reading Notes · Last updated August 2026

A repeatable three-pass process for deciding whether a paper matters, understanding its argument, and recording what is worth reusing.

First pass: establish relevance

Read the title, abstract, section headings, figures, tables, and conclusion. Identify the research question, claimed contribution, evidence type, and intended scope before spending time on technical detail.

Second pass: reconstruct the argument

Trace the chain from assumptions to method, experiment, result, and conclusion. Record unfamiliar concepts and compare the method with its strongest baseline. Use the critical reading checklist to separate demonstrated results from author interpretation.

Third pass: test and connect

Inspect equations, implementation details, ablations, limitations, and supplementary material. Ask what would happen if a key assumption changed, then connect the paper to earlier and later work.

A useful reading session ends with a concise statement of what changed in your mental model—not merely a highlighted PDF.

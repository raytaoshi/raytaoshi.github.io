Research Note-Taking and Synthesis

Paper Reading Notes · Last updated August 2026

Notes become useful when they preserve evidence, expose relationships, and can be retrieved by a future research question.

Atomic paper note

Record the citation, research question, contribution, method, data, principal result, limitations, and one-sentence takeaway. Keep direct quotations visibly separate from paraphrases.

Synthesis note

Group papers by question or mechanism rather than by reading date. Compare agreements, contradictions, evaluation settings, and open problems across sources.

Link notes deliberately

Use links to explain a relationship: supports, challenges, extends, applies, or provides a baseline for. This turns isolated summaries into a navigable research model and feeds back into the paper-reading workflow.

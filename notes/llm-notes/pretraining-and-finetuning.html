Pretraining and Fine-Tuning

LLM Notes · Last updated August 2026

Pretraining learns broad statistical structure; fine-tuning shapes that capability for tasks, instructions, and behavior.

Pretraining objective

Decoder-only language models typically minimize next-token prediction loss over large corpora. Data composition, filtering, deduplication, tokenizer design, and compute allocation all influence the learned distribution.

Supervised fine-tuning

Instruction-response examples teach the model how to express existing capabilities in a useful interface. Parameter-efficient methods can adapt a model by training a small number of added or selected parameters.

Behavioral tuning

Preference optimization uses comparisons or reward signals to favor desired responses. It complements rather than replaces careful data design and systematic alignment evaluation.

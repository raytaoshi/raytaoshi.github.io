Advanced Optimization

Advanced Deep Learning Notes · Last updated August 2026

Deep-network optimization depends on geometry, stochastic gradients, scale, and the interaction between optimizer and training schedule.

Adaptive methods

Momentum smooths noisy gradients, while adaptive optimizers rescale updates using running gradient statistics. Their practical behavior depends on epsilon, weight decay, batch size, and implementation details.

Learning-rate schedules

Warmup stabilizes early training; decay schedules reduce update magnitude later. The learning rate often has a stronger effect than switching between closely related optimizers.

Diagnosing training

Track loss, gradient norms, activation statistics, validation behavior, and throughput. Optimization changes also shape the features discussed in representation learning.

Generative Models

Advanced Deep Learning Notes · Last updated August 2026

Generative models learn a data distribution or a process for producing new samples, with different families trading off likelihood, sample quality, and generation cost.

Major families

Autoregressive models factorize a joint distribution into conditional predictions. Variational autoencoders use latent-variable inference, adversarial models learn through a discriminator, and diffusion models reverse a gradual noising process.

Conditioning and control

Labels, text, images, or structured signals can guide generation. Strong conditioning requires representations that preserve the requested attributes without collapsing diversity.

Evaluation

Likelihood, sample-based metrics, human preference, diversity, memorization, and safety measure different qualities. Evaluation should match the real use case and acknowledge metric blind spots.

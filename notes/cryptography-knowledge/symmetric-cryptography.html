Symmetric Cryptography

Cryptography Knowledge · Last updated August 2026

Symmetric schemes use shared secret key material to protect data efficiently, making key distribution and nonce discipline central concerns.

Block and stream constructions

Block ciphers transform fixed-size blocks under a key; secure modes extend them to messages. Stream ciphers generate a keystream that must never be reused with the same key and nonce.

Authenticated encryption

Confidentiality alone does not prevent tampering. Authenticated-encryption schemes protect both secrecy and integrity, and can authenticate associated metadata without encrypting it.

Operational rules

Use established libraries, unpredictable keys, mode-specific nonces, and explicit key rotation. Shared keys may be established using public-key cryptography.

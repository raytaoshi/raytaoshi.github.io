CNNs and Sequence Models

Fundamentals of Deep Learning · Last updated August 2026

Architecture encodes assumptions about data: convolution emphasizes local spatial structure, while sequence models represent ordered dependencies.

Convolutional networks

Shared filters scan local receptive fields, giving translation-equivariant feature extraction with fewer parameters than dense layers. Depth expands the effective receptive field and builds hierarchical features.

Recurrent sequence models

RNNs update a hidden state over time; gated variants such as LSTMs and GRUs improve long-range gradient flow. They process order naturally but are difficult to parallelize across time steps.

Attention-based models

Attention directly connects positions and enables parallel training. Modern language models predominantly use the Transformer architecture.
